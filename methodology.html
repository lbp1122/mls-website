Life Point Methodology

MLS is an educational and self-tracking framework, not a clinical score or medical device.

Asset Life Points (ALP)

ALP records life-building activities and behaviours. Examples may include physical activity, learning, meaningful connection, reflection and service. Activity inputs can be measured in minutes, frequency, quantity or other suitable units depending on the behaviour.

Liability Life Points (LLP)

LLP records activities or behaviours that may erode health, time, relationships, knowledge or personal values. Liability weighting may be stronger than the matching asset rate when the evidence or product logic supports a greater adverse effect.

Life Equity Points

Record what builds your life and what erodes it. Life Equity Points (LEP) are ALP minus LLP.

Each Square keeps its own ALP, LLP and LEP. MLS does not prescribe an ideal balance between Health, Belief, Knowledge and Relationship.

Health evidence

Health coefficients are informed by published guidance from leading health authorities such as WHO, AHA and CDC together with relevant peer-reviewed research. MLS converts these evidence inputs into its own Life Point model; the health authorities do not issue or endorse MLS points.

Diminishing returns and severity

More time or quantity does not necessarily produce points linearly. Where appropriate, MLS uses diminishing-return curves, caps, thresholds or severity-weighted liabilities. Repeated entries should be aggregated into the relevant daily or weekly exposure before points are calculated.

Model checks

Calculation and consistency checks help us test the model. They are not clinical validation.

Transparency

MLS aims to make the input, unit, coefficient direction and reason for each ALP/LLP contribution inspectable. The methodology will continue to be refined as the evidence base and validation work evolve.
