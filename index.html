A life ledger, not another score.

MLS uses Asset Life Points (ALP) and Liability Life Points (LLP) to show how actions accumulate across four important areas of life. The difference is your Life Equity.

ALP
+

Asset Life Points

Life-building actions such as movement, learning, meaningful connection, reflection and service.

LLP
−

Liability Life Points

Actions that erode health, time, relationships, knowledge or personal values.

Equity
=

Life Equity

Your accumulated Life Assets minus Life Liabilities — shown over time, not as a fixed ceiling.

Review changes by day, month and year while preserving the underlying activity history.

Evidence-informed. Transparent about uncertainty.

Health coefficients are calibrated from published guidance and peer-reviewed evidence. MLS then converts those inputs into its own transparent Life Point framework.

Health evidence

MLS references leading health authorities such as WHO, AHA and CDC together with peer-reviewed research. These organisations do not issue or endorse MLS points.

Stress testing

The Health model has been stress-tested across 100,000+ simulated health scenarios to check consistency, plausibility and edge cases.

No medical diagnosis

MLS is a wellbeing and self-tracking tool. Life Points are not clinical scores, biological age or medical risk predictions.

Privacy first

The current MLS test architecture keeps user-entered data on-device. MLS does not sell personal data or use health information for advertising.
