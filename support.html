Data deletion

The current MLS app does not create a remote MLS account. User-entered MLS data is designed to remain on-device. You can delete local MLS data from the app’s Privacy & Legal screen, clear app storage through your device settings, or uninstall the app.

If account or cloud functionality is introduced later, this page will be updated with the required online account/data-deletion process.

Before contacting support

  • Include your app version and test number.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened.
  • Do not email passwords, device unlock codes or sensitive medical records.

Medical issues

MLS does not provide medical advice or emergency services. If you have a medical concern, contact a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency service.
