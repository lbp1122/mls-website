Delete My Life Square Data

The current MLS app does not create a remote MLS account or remote data profile.

Delete local app data

  1. Open MLS.
  2. Go to Me → Privacy & Legal.
  3. Choose Delete all local MLS data.
  4. Confirm deletion.

You can also clear MLS app storage or uninstall the app using your device settings.

Need help?

Email mylifesquare22@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, biometric credentials or sensitive medical records.

If MLS introduces remote accounts or cloud storage in the future, this page will be updated with a web-based deletion request process before those features are released.
