Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026
Operator: Looi Boon Ping
Privacy contact: mylifesquare22@gmail.com

This policy covers the current My Life Square architecture. It will be updated before any future feature changes how personal data is collected, transmitted or shared.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how My Life Square (“MLS”) handles information when you use the app and related website.

2. Current app data handling

In the current MLS test build, Life Point entries, routines, language preferences and related app information are processed and stored locally on the user’s device. The current Android build does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not transmit MLS app data to the operator, advertisers, analytics providers or data brokers.

MLS does not sell personal information.

3. Health and wellbeing information

MLS may process information users enter about activities, nutrition, movement, habits, relationships, learning, reflection and other wellbeing-related behaviours. In the current app build, that information remains on-device.

If a future version adds Health Connect, HealthKit, wearable import, cloud backup, analytics, accounts or another external service, MLS will request permissions where required, explain the purpose of sensitive-data access, limit use to the disclosed purpose, and update this policy and the relevant store disclosures before release.

4. Biometric authentication

MLS may use the operating system’s biometric or device-credential authentication to protect access to the app. MLS does not receive or store fingerprints, face templates or other biometric identifiers. Authentication is handled by the operating system.

5. Sharing

The current app build does not transmit or share MLS user data with third parties. If third-party services are introduced later, this policy will identify the service, data involved, purpose and applicable user choices.

6. Advertising and sale of data

MLS does not use personal or health information for targeted advertising and does not sell personal information. Health or fitness data obtained through platform health frameworks will not be used for advertising, marketing profiles or unrelated data mining.

7. Data retention and deletion

Current MLS data is stored locally until you delete it using available app controls, choose “Delete all local MLS data”, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. The current app build does not create a remote MLS account.

If account creation is introduced later, MLS will add an in-app account-deletion path, an external web deletion mechanism where required, and a documented retention policy before release.

8. Security

MLS uses operating-system security controls and may use biometric or device-credential access protection where supported. No storage method is completely risk-free, so users should maintain device security and current operating-system protections.

9. Children

MLS is not designed as a child-directed service. Distribution settings, target audience and age ratings will be configured according to the actual shipping feature set in each app store.

10. Health and medical disclaimer

MLS is a wellbeing and personal-tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or medical condition.

Life Points, Asset Life Points (ALP), Liability Life Points (LLP), Life Equity and related insights are educational and self-tracking constructs. They are not clinical scores, biological age, medical risk predictions or guarantees of health outcomes.

Health-related coefficients are informed by published guidance and peer-reviewed research, but the MLS Life Point conversion is an MLS model and is not issued or endorsed by WHO, AHA, CDC or another health authority. For medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

11. International users

MLS is intended for a global audience. Privacy and data-protection rights vary by jurisdiction. MLS will comply with applicable laws in the regions where the service is offered.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when MLS changes features or data practices. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made.

13. Contact
