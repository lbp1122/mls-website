Data deletion

In MLS, open Me → Privacy & Export and choose the local-data reset option if available. The menu wording can vary by version.

On Android, you can also clear MLS app storage in device settings. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → General → iPhone/iPad Storage → My Life Square → Delete App (not Offload App).

Device backups may retain older copies of app data; manage backups separately in your operating-system settings.

If account or cloud functionality is introduced later, this page will be updated with the required online account/data-deletion process.

Before contacting support

  • Include your app version and build number, and tell us whether you use Android or iOS.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened.
  • Do not email passwords, device unlock codes or sensitive medical records.

Medical issues

MLS does not provide medical advice or emergency services. If you have a medical concern, contact a qualified healthcare professional or local emergency service.
